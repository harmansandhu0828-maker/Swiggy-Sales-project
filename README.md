🍔 Swiggy Data Analysis Project Using SQL

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Swiggy food ordering data using Microsoft SQL Server to identify business trends, customer preferences, restaurant performance, and revenue patterns.

The main objective of this project is to transform raw food ordering data into meaningful business insights using SQL queries and data analysis techniques.

Through this project, I have performed 15 different business analyses covering key performance indicators (KPIs), city-wise orders, revenue contribution, restaurant performance, cuisine preferences, customer ratings, and price-range analysis.

🎯 Project Objectives

* Calculate the total number of orders and overall revenue generated.
* Identify the average dish price and overall customer rating.
* Analyze the cities with the highest and lowest order volumes.
* Understand customer ordering patterns across different weekdays.
* Analyze yearly and quarterly order trends.
* Evaluate the revenue contribution of different states.
* Identify the top-performing restaurants based on order volume.
* Discover the most popular food categories and dishes.
* Compare cuisine performance based on orders and customer ratings.
* Analyze customer ordering patterns across different dish price ranges.

🛠️ Tools and Technologies Used

* Microsoft SQL Server
* SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)
* Microsoft Excel
* SQL

📂 Dataset Description

The Swiggy dataset contains food ordering information with the following columns:

Column Name	Description
State	Name of the state where the restaurant is located
City	Name of the city where the restaurant operates
Order_Date	Date on which the order was recorded
Restaurant_Name	Name of the restaurant
Location	Restaurant location
Category	Food category or cuisine type
Dish_Name	Name of the food item
Price_INR	Price of the dish in Indian Rupees
Rating	Customer rating
Rating_Count	Number of ratings received

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following key performance indicators were calculated to evaluate the overall business performance:

1. Total Number of Orders
2. Total Revenue Generated
3. Average Price of Dishes
4. Average Customer Rating

🔍 Business Questions and SQL Analysis

The following 15 detailed business questions were analyzed using SQL queries to understand Swiggy’s food ordering performance and identify meaningful business insights.

1. Total Number of Orders

What is the total number of food orders recorded in the Swiggy dataset across all available states, cities, restaurants, and locations?

2. Total Revenue Generated

What is the total revenue generated from all food orders recorded in the dataset, and what is the overall revenue contribution from the dishes listed in the Swiggy dataset?

3. Average Price of Dishes

What is the average price of food dishes available in the dataset across all restaurants, food categories, and locations?

4. Average Customer Rating

What is the overall average customer rating recorded in the dataset, considering only valid ratings with available rating counts?

5. Bottom 5 Cities Based on Orders

Which are the five cities with the lowest number of food orders recorded in the dataset, and how many orders have been recorded for each of these cities?

6. Top 10 Cities Based on Orders

Which are the ten cities with the highest number of food orders recorded in the dataset, and how does the order volume compare across these top-performing cities?

7. Weekday-wise Order Analysis

How are food orders distributed across the seven days of the week, from Monday to Sunday, and which days record the highest and lowest numbers of orders and revenue?

8. Yearly Order Analysis

How many food orders were recorded in each year, how much total revenue was generated annually, and how did the average dish price change over the different years?

9. Quarterly Order Analysis

How are the total number of food orders and revenue distributed across the four quarters (Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4) of each year, and which quarter records the highest order volume and revenue?

10. Revenue Contribution by States

How much total revenue is generated by each state, what percentage of the overall revenue does each state contribute, and which states contribute the highest and lowest revenue?

11. Top 10 Restaurants Based on Orders

Which are the ten restaurants with the highest number of recorded food orders, how much revenue does each restaurant generate, and what is the average customer rating of these top-performing restaurants?

12. Top Food Categories Based on Orders

Which food categories receive the highest number of recorded orders, how much revenue does each category generate, and which food categories are the most popular among customers?

13. Most Ordered Dishes

Which are the ten most frequently ordered dishes in the Swiggy dataset, how many orders has each dish received, how much revenue has each dish generated, and what is its average customer rating?

14. Cuisine Performance Based on Orders and Average Ratings

How does each cuisine category perform in terms of total number of orders, total revenue generated, and average customer rating, and which cuisine categories demonstrate the strongest overall performance?

15. Price Range-wise Order Analysis

How are food orders distributed across different dish price ranges, including below ₹100, ₹100–₹199, ₹200–₹299, ₹300–₹399, and ₹400 and above, and how much total revenue and average dish price are recorded within each price range?
